1. Introduction & Motivation1. 简介与动机

From Regression to Preference从回归到偏好

Video summarization methods typically regress frame-level importance with a mean-squared-error loss or train reinforcement-learning agents. MSE treats every error alike, so it never prioritizes the relative order between key highlights and background clips, and standard vision backbones lack a high-level understanding of a video's theme. Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) bring that semantic understanding. VSLICE asks the LVLM one binary question per frame, “Does this image show a key highlight from the video titled ‘{title}’?”, and scores the frame by the gap between its Yes and No logits.

传统视频摘要方法通常使用均方误差 (MSE) 回归帧级重要性分数，或训练强化学习智能体。MSE 对所有误差一视同仁，无法优先学习关键精彩片段与背景片段之间的相对排序；而标准视觉骨干网络也缺乏对视频主题的高层语义理解。大型视觉语言模型 (LVLM) 恰好具备这种语义理解能力。VSLICE 对每一帧向 LVLM 提出一个二元问题——“这张图像是否展示了标题为‘{title}’的视频中的关键精彩片段？”——并以 Yes 与 No 逻辑值之差作为该帧的分数。

Recognition Is Not Ranking识别不等于排序

Zero-shot LVLMs can tell a clear highlight from obvious background, but a summary is built from the top-ranked shots under a length budget, so what matters is fine-grained ordering. On pairs whose human scores differ only slightly, the zero-shot model ranks barely above chance (54.6%). Aligning it with margin-ranked binary preference optimization raises fine-grained accuracy to 75.0%, and VSLICE stays ahead across the full range of difficulty.

零样本 LVLM 能够区分明显的精彩片段与背景，但摘要是在长度预算下由排名靠前的镜头组成的，因此真正关键的是细粒度排序。在人类评分仅有细微差异的片段对上，零样本模型的排序准确率仅略高于随机水平（54.6%）。通过间隔排序二元偏好优化进行对齐后，细粒度准确率提升至 75.0%，并且在所有难度区间上 VSLICE 均保持领先。

Confidence That Means Something有意义的置信度

A useful scorer should be most accurate where it is most confident. When we keep only the pairs with the largest predicted score gap, VSLICE's accuracy rises monotonically from 77.5% to 83.9%, whereas the zero-shot model's accuracy falls from 68.3% to 64.0%. Its confidence does not track correctness.

一个可靠的打分器应当在最有把握时最准确。当只保留预测分数差最大的片段对时，VSLICE 的准确率从 77.5% 单调提升至 83.9%，而零样本模型的准确率反而从 68.3% 下降到 64.0%——它的置信度与正确性并不一致。

Fixing Errors Without Breaking What Works修正错误，而不破坏已有能力

Alignment rescues 57.7% of the pairs the zero-shot model ranked wrongly, while retaining 93.3% of the pairs it already ranked correctly. VSLICE sharpens the LVLM's existing judgement rather than overwriting it.

对齐后，零样本模型排错的片段对中有 57.7% 被纠正，同时其原本排对的片段对中有 93.3% 得以保留。VSLICE 是在强化 LVLM 已有的判断力，而不是将其覆盖。

Why a Margin?为什么需要间隔？

Standard DPO treats every chosen/rejected pair as equally different. VSLICE subtracts the ground-truth log-margin between the two clips from the implicit reward, so pairs with a larger human gap must be separated further. In training, adding the margin reaches a larger separation for less KL drift from the reference model, on both MiniCPM-V and Qwen2-VL.

标准 DPO 将每一组选中/拒绝片段对视为差异相同。VSLICE 在隐式奖励中减去两段片段之间的真实对数间隔，使人类评分差距越大的片段对需要被拉开得越远。训练中，在 MiniCPM-V 与 Qwen2-VL 上，加入间隔后都能以更小的相对参考模型的 KL 漂移获得更大的分离间隔。

2. Algorithm: Graph-DPO2. 算法：Graph-DPO

Direct Preference Optimization on Graphs图上的直接偏好优化 (DPO)

To solve the alignment issue and temporal fragmentation natively, we introduce Graph-DPO. By injecting a visual-temporal similarity graph directly into the DPO training loop, the policy learns to output aligned, temporally coherent scores without smearing false positives.

为了从根本上解决对齐问题和时间碎片化，我们引入了 Graph-DPO。通过将视觉-时间相似度图直接注入到 DPO 训练循环中，策略学习输出对齐的、时间连贯的分数，而不会涂抹误报。

  1. Ask the LVLM a binary question per frame: “Does this image show a key highlight?” and score it by the Yes/No logit gap.对每一帧向 LVLM 提出二元问题：“这张图像是否展示了关键精彩片段？”，并以 Yes/No 逻辑值差作为分数。
  2. Mine preference pairs: peak clips (chosen) vs. valley clips (rejected), with a ground-truth margin.挖掘偏好对：高分片段（选中）与低分片段（拒绝），并附带真实分数间隔。
  3. Fine-tune LoRA adapters with the preference objective against a frozen reference model.以冻结的参考模型为基准，使用偏好目标微调 LoRA 适配器。
  4. Select summary shots with knapsack dynamic programming under a length budget.在长度预算下使用背包动态规划选择摘要镜头。
