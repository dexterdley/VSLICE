1. Introduction & Motivation1. 简介与动机

The Problem: LVLM Misalignment & Overconfidence问题：LVLM 的不对齐与过度自信

Although Large Vision-Language Models (LVLMs) possess strong semantic recognition ability, their raw confidence scores are poorly calibrated for highlight localization. Reliability diagrams reveal that they suffer from massive overconfidence on non-highlight background frames, manifesting as a persistent Human Misalignment Gap.

尽管大型视觉语言模型 (LVLM) 具有强大的语义识别能力，但它们的原始置信度分数在精彩片段定位方面的校准效果很差。它们在背景帧上表现出极度的过度自信，导致了巨大的人类偏好不对齐差距 (Misalignment Gap)。

The Post-Hoc Smoothing Trap事后平滑陷阱

A naive solution to temporal fragmentation is to apply a post-hoc graph or temporal smoothing to the raw logits. However, because the zero-shot policy is fundamentally misaligned, post-hoc smoothing simply smears the overconfident false positives across adjacent frames, completely destroying precision.

解决时间碎片化的一种天真方法是对原始逻辑值进行事后的图平滑或时间平滑。然而，由于零样本策略在根本上就是不对齐的，事后平滑只会将过度自信的误报涂抹到相邻帧上，从而彻底破坏准确率。

2. Algorithm: Graph-DPO2. 算法：Graph-DPO

Direct Preference Optimization on Graphs图上的直接偏好优化 (DPO)

To solve the alignment issue and temporal fragmentation natively, we introduce Graph-DPO. By injecting a visual-temporal similarity graph directly into the DPO training loop, the policy learns to output aligned, temporally coherent scores without smearing false positives.

为了从根本上解决对齐问题和时间碎片化，我们引入了 Graph-DPO。通过将视觉-时间相似度图直接注入到 DPO 训练循环中，策略学习输出对齐的、时间连贯的分数，而不会涂抹误报。

  1. Ask the LVLM a binary question per frame: “Does this image show a key highlight?” and score it by the Yes/No logit gap.对每一帧向 LVLM 提出二元问题：“这张图像是否展示了关键精彩片段？”，并以 Yes/No 逻辑值差作为分数。
  2. Mine preference pairs: peak clips (chosen) vs. valley clips (rejected), with a ground-truth margin.挖掘偏好对：高分片段（选中）与低分片段（拒绝），并附带真实分数间隔。
  3. Fine-tune LoRA adapters with the preference objective against a frozen reference model.以冻结的参考模型为基准，使用偏好目标微调 LoRA 适配器。
  4. Select summary shots with knapsack dynamic programming under a length budget.在长度预算下使用背包动态规划选择摘要镜头。
